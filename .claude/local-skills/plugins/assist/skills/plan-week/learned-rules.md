# Plan Week Learned Rules

Corrections and calibration for how `assist:plan-week` should reason about sweeping, slotting, and presenting the week. Overrides SKILL.md. Read on every invocation.

- Always schedule tasks for a minimum of 30 minutes. It looks better on the calendar and provides buffer. Even a quick phone call gets 30m.
- Always include "Delete it" as a triage option. Not everything deserves to be relocated.
- Add an appropriate emoji prefix to tasks that lack one. Shorten task names to fit well on a calendar.
- When slotting a task, always set: date/time via `td task reschedule`, then the Scheduled label via `td task update` (remembering `--labels` replaces the whole set). The duration is set in the app; see the `td` drops `--duration` rule below.
- Todoist deadlineDate is Premium only. Note deadlines in the task description instead.
- Transition and travel conventions are in `~/Eudaimonia/Admin/Tools/google-calendar.md`. Transition is *holding space* (context shift, destination in description). Travel is *explicit* (drive / transit, destination in title). Both carry the single 🚙 Travel label (Basil); the distinction lives in the title. Transitions are always 30 minutes, never 15.
- When a Todoist bookmark is really an open question rather than an action (description phrased as a question, "Investigate" prefix, no clear next step), capture it as a koan under `~/Eudaimonia/Life-Design/Koans/<topic>.md` and delete the Todoist task. Don't punt to next Monday — questions don't get less true with time.
- Worktree mechanics for planning live in Before Every Invocation and the worktree rule below. Cut and enter the `wk-<ISO week>` worktree before any Eudaimonia or homebase edits, never mid session.
- Do not skip Plan Training (invoke `assist:plan-training` in week mode), even when training decisions feel already made inline during Sweep Calendar. The training skill carries its own gate, the previous week's retro, which has no other place to live. Surfaced 2026-05-25 when I rationalized skipping it because strength moves had been discussed during conflict resolution; Forni caught it. The retro turned up real signal (PAH as transit, PT miss, weigh-in trend) that would otherwise have stayed invisible until next Monday.
- **Walk slotting one task at a time; never bulk date the Schedule filter.** *(Presentation superseded 2026-08-09 by the board pattern entry below; the constraint that survives is never bulk act without Forni's input.)* The Slot step of Plan Tasks means presenting each task's proposed slot via AskUserQuestion (accept / move / defer / skip) and waiting for Forni before touching it. Do NOT batch reschedule the whole filter onto dates in one shot and summarize after. The collaborative per task pass *is* the process; bulk dating erases his input and forces him to re-read and unwind it. Surfaced 2026-05-31: I rescheduled 17 filter tasks at once. Forni: "you just created a bunch of tasks and put them onto dates. That's not really how we go through the scheduling process," and "you took a lot of action without asking me. Now I've got to go back and read through your summary and readjust it."
- **Default to asking before acting throughout planning, not just slotting.** Bias toward confirming each change with Forni rather than executing a batch and reporting. Calendar moves, task reschedules, deferrals — present, then wait. Same session feedback as above.
- **Do not place training sessions before the training readjustment is done.** The week's shape (which Friday it is, strength targets, any guardrail tightening) depends on the retro and any special week handling, so run Plan Training (`assist:plan-training`) before scheduling sessions. Surfaced 2026-05-31 in the FPL block: an 8 mi long run got placed before the Wk 4 retro; the readjusted number was 6 mi off the calf restart. The failure mode survives the block: scheduling ahead of the retro bakes in stale assumptions.
- **Plan in a `wk-<ISO week>` worktree, not a shared branch.** Each planning pass runs in its own worktree (see Before Every Invocation), cut in every repo it touches: Eudaimonia for planning artifacts (schedule.md, training plan, koans) and homebase for skill or config edits. A plain shared branch is not enough. On 2026-06-14 a branch switch in another terminal moved HEAD under the session mid plan and scattered commits onto an unrelated branch. A worktree gives the session its own working directory, so concurrent terminal activity cannot collide. All planning edits must target the worktree copy, not the main checkout. **Foot gun: an absolute path like `~/Eudaimonia/...` resolves to the main checkout even when the session cwd is the worktree, so edits silently land on the wrong tree.** Target the worktree path (`.claude/worktrees/wk-<ISO week>/...`). If a slip happens, move it with `git -C <main> diff -- <file>` piped to `git -C <worktree> apply`, then restore main. Surfaced 2026-06-22. Supersedes the older shared branch and date stamped approaches.
- **Green stars are walked, not auto skipped, during Sweep Inbox.** A green star means "waiting on someone else" as of the moment it was set, but the ball can quietly return: a vendor quote thread that is actually awaiting Forni's decision, or a tracker thread hiding a scheduling action. Present every starred thread for disposition rather than unilaterally classifying green stars as no action. Surfaced 2026-07-13 on the first live sweep: the skylight quote (green) was waiting on Forni's decision, and the new servicer thread (green) contained a launch call to schedule. Both were initially skipped and had to be corrected into tasks.
- **The ⏰ Scheduled label requires a specific time; label without time is an anti pattern.** Slotting means both a datetime and the label (the two Slot steps together); a task carrying the label with a date only due hides from the Schedule filter indefinitely while looking handled. The filter's `(@⏰ Scheduled & no time)` branch (added 2026-07-13) drags offenders back into triage: when one surfaces, either give it a real time or strip the label (or swap to ⏲️ Recurring for genuinely recurring reminders). Surfaced 2026-07-13: two tasks due that very day (Connect for Health enrollment, a site review) were invisible during planning, and one had already spawned an accidental duplicate.
- **Review Week presents movement as a table.** One row per activity (day, name, type, distance, vert, moving time, effort), totals as a bold summary line underneath. Requested 2026-07-19.
- **Sweep Inbox links every email to its Gmail thread.** Use the account-safe form `https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=<account>#search/rfc822msgid%3A<url-encoded-Message-ID>` (per the plugin learned-rules) in the presented board and in every question that references a message. Forni cannot act on an email he cannot open. Surfaced 2026-07-19.
- **Estimate durations honestly during slotting; 30 minutes is a floor, not a default.** After proposing slots, walk the 30 minute tasks and ask which actually need more, then reflow the day around the answers. Surfaced 2026-07-19: several 30 minute slots were clearly hour long work.
- **Present Week is a link to the Google Calendar week view** (`https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r/week/YYYY/M/D`). Todoist sync already renders scheduled tasks there, so the calendar IS the at a glance board. Do not rebuild it as prose day lists, packed summary tables, or rendered board artifacts; all three were rejected in one sitting on 2026-07-19. Supplement the link only with what the calendar cannot show: the deferred ledger, the open air summary, the week's theme.
- **Never pack multiple items into one table cell, anywhere.** One line per item, times first. Packed cells were rejected twice on 2026-07-19 (a meal grid cell carrying commentary, a week summary cramming whole days into single cells).
- **When several tasks share an undecided direction, collapse them into one planning task instead of slotting them individually.** The planning task gets the slot; the others become its unscheduled subtasks and earn slots only after the direction session. Slotting unconverged work spends the week's open air on intent that has not been decided. Surfaced 2026-07-19 with the RYLLC cluster.
- **Re-pull the calendar after any batch of deletions, before slotting; Reclaim backfills the freed time.** Clearing skeleton or one-off events (e.g. rectifying a travel week when Forni is away) triggers Reclaim to auto-reschedule its flexible task blocks into the newly freed slots. Proposing task slots off a calendar snapshot taken *before* the deletions lands them on top of the fresh Reclaim blocks. Always re-fetch the week after Sweep Calendar deletions and before the Plan Tasks slot step, and read the 🛡 Reclaim blocks as occupied. Surfaced 2026-07-26 (Wk 31, Four Pass Loop trip week): after clearing ~25 Denver skeleton events, Reclaim backfilled ATE-399/419/402 work blocks into the late mornings, and the first three slot proposals all collided until Forni asked "are you not seeing the scheduled blocks from Reclaim?" Amended 2026-08-03: the Linear task sync is retired, so the backfill risk is now habits and smart meetings rather than task blocks; the re-pull rule stands unchanged. Reaffirmed 2026-08-09: slots were again proposed off a stale pull; the re-pull applies after *any* batch of calendar writes, not only deletions.
- **The retro runs before the agent fan out.** Review Week is a quiet dialogue: the session makes its own two light pulls (Strava week, overconsumption count) and walks the retro with Forni before any background specialist is dispatched. Only when the dialogue closes do clerk, groomer, and planner launch in parallel. Surfaced 2026-08-09: background boards kept landing mid felt sense question and flooding the session; the felt sense thread cannot survive interruption.
- **The board pattern supersedes the walk one task at a time rule.** The Signal Contract (Decisions ranked with proposed defaults, plus a rollup of rule backed actions with confidence bands) replaces the per item AskUserQuestion walk from 2026-05-31 as the default presentation. Decisions stay Forni's; the presentation is a board he corrects rather than a serial interrogation, and rule backed actions execute after a phase nod, reporting in the rollup (high band grouped by action type with the rule cited, medium band listed individually for spot check). The 2026-05-31 spirit stands: never bulk act without his input, and below medium confidence is always a Decision. Adopted 2026-08-09 (refinement piece 2).
- **The landing zone was Sunday from 2026-08-09 to 2026-08-30**, when the session ran on Sunday: inbox sweep task due dates, p4 punts, slot deferrals, deferred recurring catch ups, wrap loose ends, Todoist follow ups all landed on the Sunday that planned the target week. Superseded by the Monday rule below when the session moved; kept so an older Sunday dated task reads as a landing, not an error.
- **Dispatch briefs that authorize external writes quote Forni's authorizing words verbatim, never a paraphrase.** The subagent executing the write needs the exact words that granted it; a relayed authorization restated in the dispatcher's own words tripped the security layer on 2026-08-09 and stalled the write.
- **Prefer one contiguous block per task; two at the very most, never three.** Hunt for a bigger contiguous window before you split anything: extending a weekend block by 30 minutes is cheaper than fragmenting the work across days, and a task that fits nowhere whole is a signal to make room rather than to scatter. Surfaced 2026-08-10, when a 2 point PSI pass went onto the calendar as four fragments across three days. The hours added up and the week was still unschedulable. Forni: "it's quite hard to hold in my head how that is supposed to go down." He set the bound himself: "We much prefer one contiguous block, at the very most two blocks. We wanna make sure that we have enough time to work on these things so try to find contiguous blocks if possible."
- **Verify the board before reporting it; never describe state from your own earlier actions.** Re pull Linear and the calendar and read what is actually sitting there before saying a queue is groomed, a week is settled, or nothing needs him. On 2026-08-10 the session reported a groomed queue and said "nothing needs you" while the cycle still held an untriaged issue and a 7 point item with no priority. Forni caught both. A claim about state is worth nothing without a fresh read behind it.
- **The Signal Contract has hard limits, not just principles.** Codified 2026-08-09 and violated twice on 2026-08-10, so the numbers are now explicit: present at most 8 rows or items in a single message; when more exist, present the first chunk and stop until Forni responds; ask at most one decision per message; never present a second table before the first is answered; and when he asks a question, answer only that question rather than appending the next inventory. Forni: "you're just spitting it out, spitting it out, spitting it out... There does need to be, if there's going to be human in the loop, ample room for human in the loop to actually happen here."
- **The main planning board is one consolidated message, and plain words name every item.** After the retro closes and the agent boards land, assemble everything needing Forni's input across all phases (emails, calendar, training, tasks) into a single board: grouped by category, one line per item, a proposed default on each, so one reply can settle the lot. Do not drip decisions across many small rounds; follow ups exist only for what his answers spawn. Name items by what they are ("the Tuesday swim", "ATE-470"); never invent reference codes (a "D8" labeling scheme forced "What is D8 and D9 and D10? I'm not understanding what this all is" before he could respond). Every message needing something from him closes with a bolded "Needed from you" line naming exactly what. For the main planning board this supersedes the 2026-08-10 hard limits (8 rows, one decision per message); those limits still govern everything outside the consolidated board. Surfaced 2026-08-15, week 34 planning. His words: "I'm still not getting clear action items from you in terms of what you need from me every time we go around here" and "The whole idea was for you to give me just a rundown of all the things that I need to take a look at this week... I would much prefer it was all surfaced at once versus through a bunch of different iterations."
- **Sweep every calendar in the calendarList, not only 🌱 Life.** Meetings booked through other identities live on their own calendars (the TPF calendar for `matt@theproductforge.com`), and a pull of the personal calendar alone presents their busy time as free. On 2026-08-15 the week 34 pull missed the SkySpec audit call (Wednesday 09:30, booked through the TPF Reclaim link onto the TPF calendar) and outreach got scheduled directly over it; Forni caught it, not the lint. Sweep Calendar and the planner's wide pull must fetch every calendar (`gws calendar calendarList list`, then `events list` per calendar; pin the tpf profile per `~/Eudaimonia/Admin/Tools/gws.md` for the TPF calendar), and a cross calendar event claiming working time gets a mirror block on 🌱 Life so the week reads true. **Amended 2026-08-24: the TPF calendar is out of the sweep.** Forni: "We no longer need to evaluate the TPF calendar as part of our weekly planning." The sweep covers 🌱 Life and 💻 Atelic only; the every-calendar principle survives for any future calendar that carries real busy time.
- **The Weekly Work Search block goes on the calendar before any other work is placed, every week, no exceptions.** It produces the work search activities the FY27 log records and the MyUI+ weekly payment request reports; a missed week risks the unemployment claim itself. It survives thin weeks, trip weeks, and overloaded cycles. Forni may shorten it (he cut it to one hour in week 34); dropping or trimming it is never a planning default. Codified 2026-08-15 at his explicit direction after it nearly slipped in a two day week: "That's how I collect my unemployment so we definitely need that to be scheduled and I need you to codify that."

- **Emit text only at decision points, and ask through AskUserQuestion.** During planning, status updates, board landings, and phase narration stay silent; agent results fold into the phase that consumes them. Text reaches Forni only when something needs his input, and the ask itself rides AskUserQuestion (self contained, per the plugin rules) so it is unmistakable what is needed. Codified 2026-08-24, week 35: "only output text when you need input from me... I am getting quite overwhelmed with all of the output and am never quite sure when you need input from me and what input you need."
- **The Slot step walks one decision at a time through AskUserQuestion.** Task placement is a sequential dialogue: each question carries one decision (a slot, a layout, a bundle) with a recommended default, and waits. The consolidated board still opens Plan Tasks for triage partitions (keep/defer/delete); placement itself is walked. Codified 2026-08-24: "Let's walk the tasks that need scheduling one by one." Refines the 2026-08-09 board pattern rather than replacing it.
- **One timed artifact per slot, one task per bundle.** A calendar block owns its time. Companion work lives inside the owning record: a checklist in the Linear issue behind a named block, or a comment on a single umbrella Todoist task carrying the time. Never give several Todoist tasks the same timestamp to ride a block, and never leave a bundle's components as date-only tasks either; both render as piles of chips ("We still have a bunch of tasks overlapping ... why would we be doing that?"). Collapse components into the umbrella and delete them. Codified 2026-08-24 after the RYLLC stack and the eleven estate chips.
- **Weekly planning does not touch food.** No meals phase, no pantry, no shopping day, no Cook Night (series deleted 2026-08-24). `assist:plan-meals` runs only on Forni's direct ask. Codified 2026-08-24: "Get rid of meals and food during our planning sessions please and thank you."
- **The session is the arbiter; boards never reach Forni raw.** Three boards (inbox, Linear, calendar and Todoist) landed on him at once on 2026-08-30 and he could not read any of them: "this is not consumable for me. This is too much data." The main session reads each agent's return, decides what is genuinely his, and presents one phase at a time, in order, in one short message per phase; everything else runs under the rules and is reported in a line. Agents return compact boards (decisions with a plain description, counts for the rest, the full list to a scratch file) and run on `sonnet`. Full text in SKILL.md, Mode: plan.
- **A board is the last text of its turn, and never rides in or beside an AskUserQuestion.** The dialog collapses every bit of formatting ("this just collapses all of the formatting and I can't read this"), and terminal text in the same turn as a dialog does not render before it ("You're gonna have to print out the Todoist list again. I have no idea where it is."). Board in the terminal, bolded "Needed from you" at the end, decision taken from his prose reply. The dialog is for a short fork whose whole context fits in two plain sentences. Both shapes failed in one sitting, 2026-08-30.
- **Show the thing.** An issue key, a task title, or a summary label is not something he can decide on: "What are those? You have to show them to me!" and "You have to tell me what ATE-472 is if you want me to know what to do with it." An email is sender, date, what it says, and its link; an issue is key, title, and one line of what it is. 2026-08-30.
- **The emailed retro is Review Week's first pass.** The runner's `YYYY-Www Retro` carries Strava, the takeout tally, and the outreach week already graded, so the session does not re pull or restate; it names what stands out, asks the felt sense, fills the email's own blind spots, and settles the carry forward. The headline is a grade the felt sense can flip: W35 read "skipped the plan entirely" and lived as the first weekend home after a month of racing. First run 2026-08-30.
- **Three calendars, the Todoist feed included, with offsets normalized to Denver.** A two calendar pull proposed slots over the HSA sale, the UI payment request, and a two hour estate block on 2026-08-30 ("Maybe you're not taking into account the to-do list tasks"), and the feed's `-07:00` rows read an hour early until normalized. Calendar id and the rule in SKILL.md.
- **Planning runs Monday 07:00 to 08:00, and the landing zone follows the session.** Forni, 2026-08-30: "I want it to be the first thing I do on Mondays in my deep work block." Punts, drops, and follow ups land on the next planning Monday, date only. The retro runner moved with it to Monday 05:00.
- **A fold needs its own yes.** Four house tasks were folded into the Punch List and deleted on a board reply that only said "punt punch list"; he wanted them kept ("I did not want you to fold the four house items into the punch list and delete them") and they had to be recreated. Merge only on an explicit yes to that fold. 2026-08-30.
- **`td` drops `--duration` on add and update alike.** Set durations in the app; list them under the Present Week hand list. Confirmed again 2026-08-30 on four freshly slotted tasks.
- **No fan out overlaps a dialogue, and no holding lines.** The retro and the intention both close before any agent is dispatched; the three specialists then run as one foreground wait, and the next thing Forni sees is the first phase board. When something lands while a question is open, the turn closes with nothing new; the question stays the last text. Agent results fold into the phase that consumes them, never surfaced as they arrive. 2026-09-07, after three completion notices and three holding lines landed between the intention question and its answer: "you are the marshal of what information arrives to me... be a little bit more judicious about when to show different things to me."
- **The banner carries no training block.** Set Intention writes the theme and its one sentence framing; Plan Training places events and writes nothing into the banner. Forni, 2026-09-07: "I don't look in the banner for the training block. It's not necessary."
- **High Noon and every recovery meeting carry 🤗 Community, never 🧠 Contemplation.** The label doc's coverage list was wrong until 2026-09-07 ("It's community not contemplation for the tag"); `~/Eudaimonia/Admin/Tools/google-calendar.md` now says so.
- **Cross profile gws calls in dispatch briefs say `GWS_FORCE_PROFILE=<profile>` alone.** Adding `GWS_AUTO_SWITCH=0` beside it silently pins the ambient profile (an atelic call returned the personal mailbox on 2026-09-07). The clerk brief and every mailbox instruction carry only the force variable.
