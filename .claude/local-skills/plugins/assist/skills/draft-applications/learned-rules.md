# Learned Rules

Rules earned from real applications. Append with a date; prune when superseded.

- **2026-07-28 · Palmetto is a legitimate reopen.** The Dec 2025 burn is conversation level, not company level (see Negotiation Philosophy in the Vocation CLAUDE.md). Approach fresh through a different recruiter and hiring manager, and never reference the earlier negotiation.
- **2026-07-28 · Verify remote before drafting.** Aggregator listings overstate remote (Pearl Street listed remote-unconfirmed; Palmetto showed Charlotte on Wellfound). Confirm the work style on the company's own posting before investing in a package, since full remote is a hard filter.
- **2026-07-28 · Recruiter scores arrive with the role.** The sweep's fit score and its one line rationale ride along in the shortlist; sanity check rather than re-derive, and re-score from the rubric only on disagreement.
- **2026-07-29 · Chase the aggregator to the source before drafting a word.** The "Pearl Street Staff SRE" listing resolved to its acquirer Enverus (Remote Canada only, no interconnection work) once followed to the actual ATS. Fetch the company's own posting first; it decides remote policy, level, mission ownership, and the real application questions.
- **2026-07-29 · Hub hybrid at a top tier company is Forni's explicit call.** Watershed (4 day hub) was accepted the same afternoon Palmetto (Charlotte/Boston/NYC) was declined. Present hybrid outliers with the tradeoff named; never assume the remote filter decides alone at the top of the market. The rubric carries the amended rule.
- **2026-07-29 · The sizing bands outrank peer framing.** Being an ex-CTO makes the follow-up land better at the right level; it does not move the level. The Watershed note initially targeted the co-founder CTO of a 500+ person company and was retargeted to an Engineering Leader after Forni challenged it. At 500+, aim Director or senior EM even when the peer framing is tempting.
- **2026-07-29 · Forni often drives the form himself.** The working rhythm on portal applications: produce the package (letter, answers, resume path), put the letter on the clipboard, hand over the link. Browser automation is the fallback, not the default.
- **2026-08-05 · Postings rot in days; verify at draft time and check the sibling board.** Two of five sweep roles (Euclid, Voltus Spectacle) closed in the week between sweep and drafting. Always re-verify the posting at package start, and when it is dead, read the company's live board before declaring the company dead: Voltus's closed role had a live Battery Storage sibling that became the application.
- **2026-08-05 · "No generative AI" questions belong to Forni.** When a form explicitly asks for the applicant's own words without AI (Voltus), do not draft or supply talking points. Explain the company factually so he can form a real opinion, then edit his raw dump: cut, tighten, reorder, add nothing. Interview him if he is stuck; his spoken answer is the draft.
- **2026-08-05 · Know what the company does before any drafting starts.** Forni reached a "why us" question without knowing what Voltus did. Open every package with one plain sentence of what the company actually does, before fit talk.
- **2026-08-05 · Upload-only cover letter fields get the PDF pipeline.** Letterhead HTML (name, contact line, date, body) rendered via headless Chrome to `~/Downloads/<YYYY-MM> Cover Letter - <Company>.pdf`, verified by reading the PDF back before handoff (Halter).
- **2026-08-05 · Certification-gated consultancies are usually a decline.** Salesforce partner shops gate on certified headcount (OpenTent: three certs plus nonprofit implementation years). Surface cert requirements as a leading drag in the fit read; Forni declined on sight.
- **2026-08-05 · Per-ATS access mechanics.** Ashby: the public posting API (`api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/<org>?includeCompensation=true`) is the truth about which roles are actually live; the posting page itself is a JS shell to fetchers. Lever: posting pages 403 plain fetchers but read cleanly through agent-browser, and `/apply` reveals custom form questions. Greenhouse: server rendered, fetches clean, questions visible. Breezy: JS shell, form fields invisible until a real browser opens it. Check the matching mechanic before burning fetches or declaring a posting unreachable.
- **2026-08-05 · No-AI answers assemble from the Own Words Bank.** The Vocation README's Own Words Bank holds Forni-authored sentences with provenance. For "own words, no AI" questions, edit and arrange only from bank entries; bank new sentences whenever the interview loop produces good ones.
- **2026-08-17 · Claude stages the form; Forni clicks Submit.** The working browser pattern: stage the whole ATS form via Playwright (fields, uploads, radios), verify with an accessibility snapshot readback plus a full page screenshot, then Forni clicks Submit himself in the visible window. A past tense report ("Submitted") means he did; verify and record it, never re-stage or re-submit.
- **2026-08-17 · Playwright uploads only see the worktree.** Playwright MCP file uploads accept only paths under the session worktree roots; copy rendered PDFs into `<worktree>/.playwright-mcp/` before uploading. Clean strays out of the worktree before committing, since snapshot files in the worktree root trip the markdown pre-commit lint.
- **2026-08-17 · The letterhead pipeline generalizes.** One HTML template (bold name, middot contact line, date, a "Dear `<Company>` team," greeting, the body, the "Cheers and hope to chat soon," close) rendered via headless Chrome `--print-to-pdf`, verified by reading the PDF back. Three letters shipped through it 2026-08-17.
- **2026-08-17 · No cover letter slot: append the letter to the resume.** When a form has no cover letter field (Vibrant Planet's four field Ashby form), append the letter to the resume PDF as a closing page with `pdfunite`, and verify the combined artifact visually before upload.
- **2026-08-17 · Read back every Workable field, including untouched ones.** Workable forms can arrive with a wrong prefilled address (Fort Collins appeared from nowhere). After filling, read every field back, touched or not, and decline cookie dialogs before interacting with the form.
- **2026-08-25 · Greenhouse dropdowns are react-select: click, then click the option.** Typing a value and pressing Enter fires form validation instead of selecting (every required field on Antora's form went red), and `browser_fill_form` errors because the element is not a `<select>`. The reliable pattern: click the combobox, snapshot, click the option ref. Country and city fields need the value typed slowly to wake the autocomplete, then the option clicked. Keyboard also works once a menu is open when the option order is known (Yes above No). Ashby forms are the easy case: fill_form, buttons, radios, and checkboxes all take direct hits.
- **2026-08-25 · Read the whole form before drafting a word.** Crux required a Loom demo of an LLM product plus a question driven AI screen and had no cover letter slot; Forni dropped it on sight. A gate like that is a decision to surface before any package work, not a surprise at staging time.
- **2026-08-25 · Screeners get answered from the profile, not assumed.** Antora's Python and LLM screeners and Tyba's Python years field were all Forni's calls, and a language the profile does not list is a question, never a Yes. His answer on Python (about a year) also means a Python titled role scores a notch lower on technical fit at sweep time.
- **2026-08-25 · The website is mattforni.com.** `home.mattforni.com` redirects but never gets written; the "put a face on the application" link is `https://mattforni.com/career`.
- **2026-08-25 · Fan the hiring manager research out the moment the picks land.** One researcher per company, in parallel, while Forni reads the board, so every package arrives with its person and email pattern attached. When an ad names the search firm contact (Denver Water named Giddings Group's Pete McManus), that named recruiter is the door, not the CEO.
- **2026-08-12 · Links live in the wording.** A follow up never carries a bare URL; the role, the company page, and the career page are hyperlinked on the descriptive words themselves, the same rule Slack posts follow.
- **2026-08-12 · Consult the Vocation voice samples before drafting a word.** `~/Eudaimonia/Craft/Vocation/Voice/` (the folder that was `samples/`) holds the job search writing samples and its README indexes the voice gems elsewhere in Vocation; read it before any letter, answer, or follow up, per its own CLAUDE.md.
- **2026-08-12 · `firstname.lastname@` beats `firstname@` at 150 plus person companies.** Small companies hand out `firstname@`; once headcount passes roughly 150 the pattern flips, so lead with `firstname.lastname@` there and keep the two bounce stop.
- **2026-09-08 · Rippling forms have their own mechanics.** `fill_form` takes the text fields; selects are click, snapshot, then click the option; the location field autocompletes on slow typing and then needs its option clicked; uploads go through the "Drop or select" button. The disability question's option wording differs per ATS, so read the options rather than matching a remembered label.
- **2026-09-08 · An "account is inactive" bounce means the person left.** A 550 5.2.1 "account is inactive" bounce is a different signal from "address not found": the mailbox existed and was disabled, which usually means the person left the company. Redirect the note to the next verified candidate in band rather than spending the second guess on an address variant (Camus: `david@` bounced inactive, resent to the CEO).
- **2026-09-08 · Email only postings get a gws Gmail draft.** When a posting takes applications only by email (Kevala), the application is a gws Gmail draft to the careers address with the posting's required subject, the resume and letter attached as PDFs named for the applicant. `-a` paths must sit at the shell's working directory root, so stage the files there, create the draft, then delete them. Send only on Forni's yes, and log it as an application with the email as the method.
- **2026-09-08 · Standing form answers live in work-history.md.** The Standing Form Answers table there holds the eligibility screeners and voluntary self identification answers; the EnergyHub form on 2026-09-08 was the last one that asked.
- **2026-09-08 · Record an email pattern the moment a send delivers.** EnergyHub is `firstname.lastname@` (delivered twice). Per aggregators, `firstname@` leads at Camus Energy and `firstname.lastname@` at Kevala.
