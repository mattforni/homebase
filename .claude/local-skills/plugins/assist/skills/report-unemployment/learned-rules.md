# Learned Rules

Rules earned from real MyUI+ filings. Append with a date; prune when superseded.

- **2026-08-11 · The dropdown ids are swapped relative to their labels.** The type of activity select is `ddlContactmethod` and the contact method select is `ddlContactType`. Map by element id, never by name.
- **2026-08-11 · Text field ids are stable across entries.** `#txtDate` (MM/DD/YYYY), `#txtEmployerAgencyNA`, `#txtContactInformation`, `#txtAddlContInfo`. Snapshot refs go stale after every save; re-grab the Save this Work-Search ref each time, and confirm the save by the numbered list growing.
- **2026-08-11 · The page's Errors heading is not an error.** It labels the claimant info block (name, claimant ID, claim ID). Real validation failures appear inline at the field.
- **2026-08-11 · Hidden dialog templates litter every page.** Inactivity warning, session expired, and nightly processing modals sit in the DOM permanently; ignore them unless they are actually visible.
- **2026-08-11 · The bundled Chromium cannot pass the ID.me Cloudflare challenge.** Headed mode with a human clicking still spins forever on the automation fingerprint. Attaching over CDP to a real browser with a persistent profile passed it, in Brave until 2026-09-30; the flow now runs on the headed personal Chrome identity, which is the same shape and passes too.
- **2026-08-11 · Pin the tab before filling.** An attached session follows Forni's focused tab, so his mid-entry HubSpot browse retargets fill commands. `tab list`, then `tab <id> --pin-tab`, immediately after opening MyUI+.
- **2026-08-11 · The flow can resume mid-stream.** Sections already completed (Basic Questions from an earlier attempt) show Complete on the Weekly Payment Request hub and are not re-asked. Read the hub state before assuming a fresh start, and read completed answers back at the Summary gate.
- **2026-08-17 · Button ids.** `btnWorkSearchYNNext` (activity count Next), `btn_Next` (activity list Next and plan Next; `btnWorkSearchLogNext` is not clickable), `btnWorkSearchLogAdd2` (Save this Work-Search), `prevZeroBtn` (Previous on the first wizard step), `btn_Previous` (back one step inside the wizard, form intact). Prefer these stable ids over snapshot refs, which shift on every postback; a "Could not locate element" right after a click usually means the page already navigated, so check `tab list` first.
- **2026-11-09 · Navigate the hub by `div[data-btn-clickid=X]`: `EARN` Earnings, `WSCH` Work Search, `CONF` Summary.** The Not Started badges and the headings are decorative. As of 2026-11-09 a plain `click` on the div did nothing; a DOM click on its inner button in `eval` (`document.querySelector("div[data-btn-clickid=EARN] button").click()`) opened the section.
- **2026-08-17 · The employer name field rejects parentheses and caps at 30 characters.** Allowed specials are `,.'#@-/&`. A violation fails the save silently: the numbered activity list does not grow, and the reason sits only in an error span reading "is not a valid Entity Name", so read the error spans with `get html` before retrying. Worked substitutions: `EarthRanger - Ai2`, `Intuito for Takt`, `True Search for Signalcore`.
- **2026-08-17 · Entering from Claim Status walks four screens a mid flow resume never shows.** Full order: My Claim Status, the specific week row's start link, an intro page, **Address Verification** (read the mailing and residential address, Confirm only if correct, never Update unless it genuinely changed), Basic Questions instructions, then the questions.
- **2026-11-09 · Basic Questions is one five step wizard: Instructions, Work, Employment, Payments, Able and Available.** NEXT advances each step and the last button is FINISH, which closes the section only and certifies nothing. Radio id groups under `ctl00_ctl00_cphMain_cphMain_`, suffix `_0` Yes and `_1` No: Work `radDidWork`, `radDidHldy`; Employment `radOfferedEmployment`, `radRefusedEmployment`, `radRefusedDueToAnother`, `radQuitJob`, `radDischarged`, `radLaidOff`; Payments `radReceivedIncome`; Able and Available `radAbleToWork`, `radAvailableToWork`, `radJobContact`.
- **2026-08-17 · Report every genuine activity, not just the five the cadence targets.** Week 34 had nine (five applications, four networking contacts) and all nine went in; the count radio tops out at "Five or More" either way. Hiring manager follow ups (kind `follow_up`) stay folded into the application they follow rather than counted twice.

- **2026-11-09 · `eval` runs under the prefix allow rule.** It drove the whole 2026-11-09 filing, reads and writes alike. Wrap each script in an arrow function, since a bare top level `const` collides with the previous call's declaration. The 2026-08-25 denial predates the prefix rule.
- **2026-08-25 · The dropdown value codes, so no option text has to be read or matched.** Type of activity (`ddlContactmethod`): `AJSS` seminars and career networking meetings, `JAPE` completed a job application, `RWPE` registered with a private employment agency or placement service, `RWES` registered with Colorado Workforce Office, `RUHL` union hall, `RJLI` reviewed job listings, `PSAO` skills assessments, `PIWW` instructional workshop, `OTHA` other job search activities, `OFJR` followed up on job referrals, `NWCF` networked with colleagues or friends, `INTP` interviewed with a potential employer in person or by telephone, `INPV` in person visit with an employer, `CPAA` contacted professional associations, `UOJM` used online job matching systems, `USEM` used Workforce Office employment resources. Contact method (`ddlContactType`): `CEMA` Email, `CFAX` Fax, `CMAI` Mail, `CINP` In Person, `CPHN` Phone, `ONLN` Online. Outcome (`ddlResults`): `APLD` Applied, `NODE` No Decision, `HIRE` Hired, `NTHI` Not Hiring, `PEND` Pending, `INTR` Interviewed, `INDS` Interview Date Set, `NORS` No Response from Employer, `WKSA` Work-Search Activity, `NAPL` Not Applicable.
- **2026-08-25 · Read a dropdown's options in full or not at all.** A partial extraction reported `INTP` as absent and sent a needless question to Forni about which type to use. There are 16 type options and they do not render in the order the skill's mapping table implies. Parse the select's HTML with a real regex over every `<option value=...>` pair rather than eyeballing a truncated dump.
- **2026-08-25 · Check each field's ceiling with `get attr <sel> maxlength` before filling.** `txtDate` 12, `txtEmployerAgencyNA` 30, `txtContactInformation` 50 and truncates silently, and `txtAddlContInfo` carries no attribute but holds to 250, so count before filling it. A long posting URL goes in Additional Information, with a short, still valid board URL in the contact field (`https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/texture`).
- **2026-08-25 · The Edit button on a saved activity is jQuery bound; `#btnWorkSearchLogEdit` does nothing.** Click `.btnEditWorkSrch` instead, which loads that activity into the form. In edit mode the buttons change from "Save this Work-Search" to Save and Delete, and clicking Save commits and returns to a blank new entry form without duplicating the row. Verify the row count did not grow before moving on.
- **2026-08-25 · `get html` returns the static attribute, not live state; read checkboxes with `is checked`.** After checking the five plan boxes, `get html` showed all of them unchecked while `is checked` correctly returned true. Never conclude a check failed from the HTML alone.
- **2026-08-25 · `chkEnhancedConfirmation` is the work search certification checkbox.** Full id `#ctl00_ctl00_cphMain_cphMain_chkEnhancedConfirmation`. It is present in the DOM from the plan step onward, well before the gate. Claude never checks it, with or without his yes; it is his hands. The plan checkboxes share the prefix: `chkInquiredPlanOnline`, `chkAppliedPlanOnline`, `chkInterviewedPlanOnline`, `chkInterviewedPlanByPhone`, `chkBoxOtherActivities`.
- **2026-08-25 · An ID.me "STOP AND READ" interstitial is not an expired session.** It asks "Are you verifying your identity to access state benefits or government services?" with a single Yes button, and clicking it redirects straight back into the claimant portal on an already valid session. A background run bailed to Forni for a password that was never needed. Click Yes and re read the tab before concluding a fresh login or MFA is required.

- **2026-10-05 · Claude fills the whole request; the certifications stay Forni's.** The allow rule `Bash(agent-browser --session myui --identity personal --headed:*)` in user settings is what clears the permission classifier for this flow, so every command must begin with exactly that prefix. With it, Claude confirms the address, answers Basic Questions with the standing answers (No to work only when Forni confirms no practice hours and no deal or invoice that became final in the claim week, see SKILL.md step 2; No to holiday, every employment question, and income; Yes to able, available, and job contact, unless he says otherwise that week), and enters the activities and the plan. A rule covering only `click` was not enough: the next read was denied as a bypass. Without the rule the classifier denies these clicks even on his explicit yes, and that denial is a hard stop. The deny rules `Bash(agent-browser *chkEnhancedConfirmation*)` and `Bash(agent-browser *chkSelfEmployment*)` keep both certification checkboxes out of Claude's reach.
- **2026-09-07 · Forni's manual click lands the flow in a brand new tab and the pin does not follow.** After he clicked Start Payment Request, the session's pinned tab was gone from `tab list` entirely, the flow was on a fresh tab id at `InitialQuestions.ASPX`, and several unrelated tabs had closed. `snapshot` against the stale target returned empty output rather than an error, which reads exactly like a dead session. Re-list tabs and re-pin before concluding anything is broken.

- **2026-11-09 · Set the searchable dropdowns in the page, not with the mouse.** Type of Activity and Outcome sit behind chosen widgets; set them with `$("#ctl00_ctl00_cphMain_cphMain_ddlContactmethod").val(code).trigger("change").trigger("chosen:updated")` (and the same for `ddlResults`) in `eval`, then read back each select's value and its `.chosen-single` text. Contact method is a plain select set the same way. A radio or button that a `click` does not stick (the Earnings radios, `btn_Next`) takes `el.click()` in `eval`. Fill the employer last and still read back all seven values before each Save (the 2026-10-12 rule). The mouse press method survives in git history (this file at commit 8a457286, the last version before the rewrite) as the fallback if `eval` is ever refused.

- **2026-09-28 · Put the identity flag on every command; a bare `connect <port>` launches its own bundled Chromium.** The only sign is a "launched browser" line and a tab list holding just about:blank. Close the stray browser and use `--identity personal --headed` throughout (it was `--cdp 9222` in the Brave era).
- **2026-10-05 · The week row link and Start Payment Request (`btnRequestBenefits`) both respond to a plain ref click.** The 09-28 failure did not reproduce.
- **2026-09-28 · Additional Information rejects `_`.** A Workday posting URL carries underscores and fails the save; enter the job id and the careers site URL instead.
- **2026-09-28 · In a background tab, scrolling advances only while frames are pumped.** `get box` is accurate only after repeated screenshots stop the position moving.

- **2026-10-05 · `scrollintoview` does not move this page; use `scroll down <px>` and pump screenshots until `get box` stops changing.** The form sits below the fold after every save, so re read the box each time.
- **2026-10-05 · The headed personal Chrome identity passes the ID.me Cloudflare check.** Forni signs in and clears MFA; after that, his hands are needed only at the certifications and for the Basic Questions and Earnings answers that are his.
- **2026-10-05 · Write the full command every time.** A command prefix held in a zsh variable does not word split and fails as command not found, and it would also miss the allow rule's prefix.

- **2026-10-12 · Chrome autofill can hijack a dropdown click and write "Atelic" into the employer field.** The searchable dropdown's search box is a text input, so Chrome sometimes floats an autofill suggestion over the option list; the press meant for the option lands on the suggestion, the type select stays empty, and `txtEmployerAgencyNA` silently becomes the saved organization name. It happened on two of six entries. Before every Save, read back all seven values (date, employer, type, contact method, contact information, outcome, additional information) and compare each to what was intended; save only on a full match. Fill the employer field last, after both dropdowns are set, and set again a dropdown whose value does not equal the intended option rather than moving on. Any retry voids the readback, so refill the employer and read all seven values again; after two failed retries stop and bail to Forni.
- **2026-10-12 · The form lives in an inner scrolling pane, so `btn_Next` can sit far below the viewport.** `get box` returned y 1273 in a 779 pixel viewport after a 600 pixel scroll, and the press landed on nothing. Press it with `el.click()` in `eval` (the 2026-11-09 dropdown rule), which needs no scroll; on the mouse fallback, scroll until the whole box reads inside the viewport and press only then.
- **2026-10-12 · Read the confirmation before the window goes away.** Forni submitted and closed the window, the bound tab was gone, and a fresh tab landed on the login page, so the confirmation number needed a second sign in. At the gate, tell him in the handoff message to leave the confirmation page open after he submits; the stamp cannot be written without the number.
- **2026-10-12 · Able, available, and work search are Yes every week, with no exception for a medical event.** The claim week of 10-04 held a gum graft surgery and Claude paused on the able and available screen to ask; his answer was "Yes to all of them, always." Do not ask about those three, and do not present the calendar as a reason to. As of 2026-11-09 the claim closes with the claim week 2026-11-08 to 11-14, filed 2026-11-16; 11-09 filed as a regular request with practice work reported (the Earnings rules below), and the close files the same way, with the work question asked of Forni first (SKILL.md step 2).
- **2026-10-12 · When the confirmation number is lost, stamp the ledger with a labeled note.** The portal's status page, payment history, and inbox do not show the number after the fact; they show the week as Submitted. Stamp the activities with a sentence saying no number was captured and what the portal showed on what date, so the rows do not read as unreported, and never invent a code.

- **2026-10-25 · Postbacks take seconds, and a click that seems to do nothing has often landed.** Wait about four seconds and pump screenshots before retrying. A button with a recorded id is still driven by that id; for one without (the Address Verification Confirm, the Basic Questions NEXT), where a `@ref` click did nothing, `find role button click --name "<label>"` worked, run behind the usual `agent-browser --session myui --identity personal --headed` prefix.
- **2026-10-25 · The deny rules on the certification checkbox ids refuse reads as well as clicks.** Never name either id in any command, `is visible` included; read the confirm step from a `snapshot`.

- **2026-11-02 · Split per activity rows with `IFS='^' read -r`, never a zsh only expansion.** A loop using `${(@s:^:)act}` was refused whole by the built in rm safety check as a command it could not parse, though it held no removal. The portable `read` split ran clean.

- **2026-11-09 · Yes to work opens an Earnings section on the hub.** The form defines work as any activity paid or to be paid, self employment included, so practice hours in the claim week make the answer Yes. Screen one: `txtGrossWages` (W2 wages only, $0 when the practice pays no salary), `ctl00_ctl00_cphMain_cphMain_rblPtoEarnings`, `txtHours`, `ctl00_ctl00_cphMain_cphMain_rbSelfEmployed`, `ctl00_ctl00_cphMain_cphMain_rblReturnToWork`. Screen two: `txtSelfEmpEarnings` and `txtGigEmpWageAm`; `txtTotalSelfGigWageAm` recomputes only after input, keyup, change, and blur events are dispatched on both. Its "I certify that I have read and understood" checkbox (`chkSelfEmployment`) is Forni's, behind the same kind of deny rule as the work search box.
- **2026-11-09 · Page scripts in `eval` never touch a checkbox.** The deny rules match only a certification id written into the command, so a script that finds a box another way would pass them; they are a tripwire, and this rule is the guard. Plan boxes are the one exception, set by their recorded ids.
- **2026-11-09 · Self employment earnings count in the week a deal becomes final, collected or not.** The form's own wording: the week a product sells or a transaction closes or becomes final, regardless of payment. A retainer counts in the week it is invoiced; time and materials work counts as that week's billable hours times the rate. Hours and earnings are Forni's answers; bring him the Toggl totals and the agreements' terms as the question.
- **2026-11-09 · The plan step refuses to advance with no box checked** ("At least one work search plan selections are required"). Check only what is genuinely planned, an interview already on the calendar included; when nothing is, the honest move is to stop and ask Forni, who decides whether a plan is real; the final close request is the one he has already decided to file.
- **2026-11-09 · The confirmation page drops an empty `download.html` into the session's working directory.** Removing it was blocked as irreversible; name it in the wrap for Forni to delete.
